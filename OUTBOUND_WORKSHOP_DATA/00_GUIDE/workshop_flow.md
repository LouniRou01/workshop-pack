# Workshop flow

1. Select an account, contact, and signal.
2. Research the account and contact using the evidence files.
3. Produce a research brief and identify a credible outbound angle.
4. Draft a campaign and optional landing-page brief.
5. Classify replies and propose the next action.
6. Review CRM state before proposing any update.

The core exercise is read-only. External sending and CRM writes require human approval.
